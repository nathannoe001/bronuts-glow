# BRONUTS Glow

"Act as a world-class web designer and developer. Build a modern, high-converting website for a gourmet donut shop and cafe named BRONUTS.

Business Data:

Brand Name: BRONUTS

Focus: Gourmet Handmade Donuts, Artisanal Glazes & Specialty Coffee

Rating: 4.9 Stars from 185+ Google Reviews

Design Requirements (Premium Glow Aesthetic):

Color Palette: Deep Dark Mode background (#0A0A0A) with vibrant neon glaze accents (warm golden amber #FFB800 and neon magenta #FF007A). Apply a subtle 2% Noise Texture Overlay across the site.

Sticky Navbar: High-end Glassmorphism (blurred dark background, 1px subtle white border) with logo 'BRONUTS', links ('Menu', 'Specialty Flavors', 'Reviews', 'Locations'), and a glowing CTA button ('Order Online').

Hero Section: Dynamic Text Masking headline reading 'Freshly Glazed Obsession' with a rolling gradient inside the text. Below the headline, include a ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.9-Star Google Rating badge and dual Magnetic Buttons ('Explore Menu' and 'Order Pickup').

Menu Highlights (Bento Box Layout): Asymmetric grid displaying signature donuts (e.g., Chocolate Lava Glaze, Salted Caramel Crunch, Pistachio Dream, Classic Vanilla Sprinkle). Each bento card must use High-End Glassmorphism with Intentional Micro-Interactions (5px lift on hover and glowing border spotlight following cursor position).

Marquee Scrolling Banner: Infinite hollow scrolling text banner above the footer reading: 'FRESHLY BAKED DAILY • HANDMADE GOURMET DONUTS • SPECIALTY COFFEE • ORDER ONLINE •'

Visual Showcase: Parallax Image Reveals for high-res donut photography (images expand and pan slightly on scroll).

Social Proof: Testimonial Wall featuring Google Reviews in frosted glass cards that slide in on scroll.

Lead Capture / Order Form: 'Order Pickup or Delivery' section wrapped in glassmorphic cards with glowing input borders on click.
